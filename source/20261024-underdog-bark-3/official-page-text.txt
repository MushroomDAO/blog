Bark 3.0 Clean writes what you meant
People don’t talk the way they write. Bark 3.0 Clean leaves out “um” and “uh”, filler like “you know” and “I mean”, false starts and repeated words. So “Um, I don-, I can’t think off my head” comes out as “I can’t think off my head.”
It’s the version Underdog uses by default, for dictation and for meeting notes. If you want every word, pick Bark 3.0 or Bark 3.0 Plus in Speak.

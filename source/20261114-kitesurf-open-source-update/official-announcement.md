# Official open-source announcement — research summary

Source: https://blog.cloudflare.com/kitesurf-open-source/
Published: 2026-11-13
Full fetched text retained privately in the task job; navigation and third-party footer content excluded from public evidence.

Cloudflare released Kitesurf under Apache 2.0, enabling source inspection, modification, local development and deployment to an owner's Workers account. Kitesurf separates session/client control, page execution and rendering. Its ecosystem includes Blitz, Stylo, Boa, Linebender components and Krilla, with third-party notices preserved.

The announcement describes local development and account deployment. Cloud deployment needs Workers Paid for Dynamic Workers. Browser compatibility, efficiency and reliability remain active development areas. The release is an implementation and experimentation opportunity, not evidence of complete Chrome compatibility or free cloud compute.

The original introduction motivates a Workers-based browser for agent workloads, splitting browser responsibilities into isolated runtime components and combining Rust/WebAssembly with JavaScript execution. It reports resource measurements on a particular sample corpus and discusses development progress. Those early figures were not reproduced in this revision and are not used to infer proportional concurrency, pricing or universal compatibility.

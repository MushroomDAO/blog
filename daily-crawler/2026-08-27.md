# SME AI Daily Brief — 2026-08-27

## Today’s strongest signal

Enterprise agents are increasingly being packaged as **skills + company data + workflows + governance**, while pricing is moving toward completed work rather than raw tokens.

## Top topics

### 1. Sell completed work, not tokens

SME owners do not care how many tokens an agent consumed. They care how many emails, leads, cases, orders or calls were handled.

**Product model:**

`Model/API cost → Workflow/Skill → Business Work Unit → Customer-facing usage`

Examples:

- emails processed
- leads qualified
- follow-ups completed
- support cases handled
- voice minutes completed

**Open-source component:** `usage-meter` with `input → action → result → cost → work_unit`.

**Paid layer:** Billing, budget alerts, optimization, business-unit pricing.

**Content:** Strong commercial strategy Blog.

### 2. SME Agent should look like a Skills runtime

Instead of one giant general agent, the node should expose reusable capabilities:

- Email
- CRM
- Quote
- Follow-up
- Marketing
- Invoice
- Customer Service

**Open-source component:** Skill spec, registry, installer, permission manifest.

**Paid layer:** Vertical Skill Packs and managed skills.

**Project to study:**

- Digital Marketing Pro: https://github.com/indranilbanerjee/digital-marketing-pro

It is useful as a reference for how a large marketing capability can be decomposed into many reusable skills.

### 3. Who manages five AI employees?

When a small company starts running Sales, Marketing, Support, Finance and Research agents, the problem becomes organizational rather than purely technical.

**Needed controls:**

- Agent registry
- Task assignment
- Budgets
- Permissions
- Human approvals
- Audit
- Goal alignment

**Project to study:**

- Paperclip: https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip

**Open-source component:** Agent registry + task + budget + approval + audit.

**Paid layer:** Managed Agent Control Plane.

### 4. Marketing Agent should become a Marketing OS

The free layer should go beyond “generate a post” and include brand context, poster/banner generation, repurposing, SEO and review monitoring.

**Open-source component:** Brand context + Marketing Skill Pack + Human Approval.

**Paid layer:** Campaign agent, competitor monitoring, lead attribution, ads optimization, weekly growth reporting.

## Recommended action

**Blog:** “Don’t sell AI by token: SMEs want to buy completed work.”

**Product issue:** `AI Work Unit / Usage Meter v0.1`.

This bridges pricing, product telemetry and ROI.
